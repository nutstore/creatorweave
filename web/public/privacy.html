Privacy Policy

English · 简体中文

This policy applies to the eo2weave browser extension ("the extension") offered by Astronet Technology Pte. Ltd. ("we", "us").
Last updated: August 17, 2026.

Summary

  • We do not operate a server that receives your data. The extension does not upload your conversations, files, or browsing activity to us.
  • Page content is read only when you or your AI agent explicitly ask for it.
  • Page content is sent to your chosen AI model provider only as part of the tasks you explicitly run, never in the background.
  • Website tool access (WebMCP) is granted per site and can be revoked at any time.
  • No ads, no trackers, no selling of data.

1. Data we process

The extension runs locally in your browser. To do its work, it processes the following information, on your device:

  • Page content. When you or your agent ask a question about a page, or ask the agent to read a specific page, the extension extracts the content of that page for the task.
  • Page interactions. With your consent, the agent may perform actions on a page (for example clicking a button or filling a form). These actions are performed only for tasks you initiate.
  • Local state. Settings, per-site authorization grants, and cached tool descriptions are stored in the extension's local storage on your device.
  • Search queries. When you ask the agent to search the web, the query is passed to the search backend needed to fulfill that request.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not collect or upload your browsing history.
  • We do not read pages in the background; content is processed only for tasks you explicitly run.
  • We do not send your data to servers operated by us.
  • We do not include ads or third-party trackers, and we do not sell or share your data.

3. AI model providers

To answer your requests, the extension sends the relevant task content (which may include page content you asked about) to the AI model provider you configure (for example, an API provider you supply a key for). That content is processed under the provider's own privacy policy. This happens only when you run a task — never automatically in the background.

4. Website tools (WebMCP)

When a website you visit exposes tools via WebMCP, the extension lists them for you. Using those tools requires your explicit per-site authorization, which you can revoke at any time. Tool calls are routed between your browser tabs only.

5. Optional desktop helper

The extension can optionally integrate with a separately installed desktop helper that lets the agent read and write files in folders you designate. The helper is never installed silently; the extension works fully without it.

6. Storage and deletion

All local state (settings, authorizations, caches) stays on your device. Removing the extension deletes it.

7. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the extension. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
